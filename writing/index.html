    A Provider’s Perspective of a Compute Exchange

    AI compute is scarce and intrinsically valuable, but a lack of standardization and divisibility keeps it from being traded the way oil and gold are. This white paper models GPU resource allocation from a provider’s point of view: pricing an ask against competing bids, the cost of sampling utilization slowly, energy and compute prices as coupled stochastic processes, and a correlation metric for a tokens market.

    Diffusion from First Principles

    Diffusion is a physical term before it is a machine learning one. This text builds up the forward SDE, the Fokker-Planck equation, the reverse SDE, the Boltzmann distribution, the Einstein-Stokes relation, Langevin sampling, and the fluctuation-dissipation theorem, starting from a speck of dust in water.
